# Benchmarks are REDS4 and a frozen 30-clip Vimeo subset

REDS results use only REDS4 (clips 000, 011, 015, and 020), not the official validation or test splits. The public REDS test split has no ground truth, and the validation split is a different 30 clips. Vimeo-90K results use 30 seven-frame clips drawn once from the official test list; the list is stored in the repo and not redrawn. The two benchmarks are reported separately. The full training sets stay out of scope until this sample pipeline runs.
